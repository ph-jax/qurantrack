# Staff administration

System administrators and organization administrators manage staff only in the organization selected by their authenticated server session. Organization administrators cannot assign or alter `system_admin`; nobody can change their own membership or remove the final active administrator.

The Staff page lists active/inactive memberships and invitation history. Resending a pending invitation invalidates its prior link. Revocation is immediate. Delivery failures remain safe to retry. Stored `teacher` roles are shown as **Teacher / Mentor** in English and **Öğretmen / Mentor** in Turkish.

Phase 3B1 is limited to memberships, invitations, roles, and switching. Phase 3B2 adds groups/classes, teacher assignments, rosters, enrollments, and server-enforced teacher visibility. Phase 3C adds approved default curriculum and program administration. Phase 3D adds new-organization onboarding.
